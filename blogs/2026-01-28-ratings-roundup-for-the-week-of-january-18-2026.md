CBS ended the pins-and-needles waiting for many of its fans last week. The Amazing Race, Elsbeth, Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage, Fire Country, Matlock, all three of the current NCIS shows, Survivor, and Tracker all received renewal notices. Conspicuously absent from that roster is Watson, and freshman shows DMV and The Road.
